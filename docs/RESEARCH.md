# Commercial Cabinets Marketplace: evidence and product decisions

Research checked November 1, 2026. This is a bounded product brief, not a market-size study. Statements about vendors below are sourced to their own documentation unless explicitly marked otherwise. Vendor marketing does not independently establish outcomes. No search-volume, conversion-rate, supplier-price, or revenue estimates are asserted.

## Cabinet-specific comparables and vocabulary evidence

The sampled cabinet businesses are suppliers or distributors, not proven independent multivendor marketplaces. [Cosmo Cabinets](https://www.cosmocabinets.com/) uses multifamily, affordable housing, senior living, student housing, turnkey service, kitchen and bath, and distributors. [ASCH](https://www.aschfm.com/multi-family-housing) emphasizes turnkey cabinets and countertops and shows project examples. [Cabinet Specialties](https://www.cabspec.net/multi-unit-housing/) emphasizes apartment upgrades, stock availability, installation and minimal tenant disruption. These are evidence of industry vocabulary, not proof of buyer query volume or supplier qualification.

AWI provides stronger specification vocabulary: shop drawings, material data, finish samples, core materials, hardware, casework elevations, field dimensions and separately identified substitutions. [AWI submittals purpose](https://awinet.org/standards/submittals/1-0-2-0-submittals/), [manufacturer responsibilities](https://awinet.org/standards/requirements-category/manufacturer-supplier-responsibility/). Use these as RFQ prompts; do not assert that all suppliers or products meet a standard.

## Complaints and risk evidence

- The FTC finalized a 2023 HomeAdvisor consent order addressing misleading lead claims, with up to $7.2 million in redress. This is a documented historical enforcement event about HomeAdvisor (doing business as Angi Leads), not evidence that every current lead product behaves that way. [FTC case record](https://search.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/1923106-homeadvisor-matter). **Apply:** explicit consent, recipient audit trails and no unsupported conversion claims.
- Houzz's own cancellation documentation says most plans run for 12 months and generally cannot be canceled mid-contract; notice requirements apply to nonrenewal. This establishes contractual friction, not a measured complaint prevalence. [Cancellation terms](https://pro.houzz.com/pro-help/r/how-to-cancel-your-houzz-pro-subscription). **Apply:** transparent eventual monetization terms and renewal controls.
- Public forum complaints about nonresponsive paid leads and cancellation trouble are anecdotal and selection-biased. They were not used to assert failure rates. No bounded, comparable complaint dataset was established for Thomasnet, Alibaba or BuildingConnected. **Next research:** interview buyers and suppliers about recent actual procurement events.

## Recommended launch choices

1. Acquire buyers through useful sector guides, filterable capability pages and a transparent editable budget calculator. Directory and category URLs are observable discovery structures; the competitors' organic traffic contribution is unverified.
2. Recruit suppliers through a free application, manual review, proof-based profiles and useful RFQ response tracking. Begin with geographic/capability coverage rather than a fabricated large inventory.
3. Use a common RFQ schema and comparison table; commercial value comes from scope clarity, delivery feasibility and total installed budget, not a superficial lowest-price rank.
4. Launch monetization as clearly disclosed featured placements only when authorized; subscription/qualified-lead/referral/transaction models remain hypotheses until willingness to pay and attribution are validated. No billing is needed for the local MVP.
5. Publish one substantial page per sector with different procurement concerns, checklist and CTA defaults. Use canonical URLs, meaningful titles/descriptions, crawlable content and a sitemap. Exclude admin, private RFQs and demonstration suppliers from indexing.
6. Measure real events: search, shortlist, RFQ submitted, supplier response and buyer-confirmed award. Do not present synthetic activity as traffic or conversion reporting.
